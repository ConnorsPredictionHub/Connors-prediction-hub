About Connor's Prediction Hub

Connor's Prediction Hub is a private sports analytics workspace bringing together dedicated prediction models for hockey, football, baseball, basketball, horse racing and greyhound racing.

Our focus is clear presentation: upcoming matchups, model-generated estimates, ranked selections and a personal bet tracker in one place. Model outputs are estimates, not guarantees, and some automated calculations remain provisional while their data and calibration are developed.
